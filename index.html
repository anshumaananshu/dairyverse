Assignment structure generator

Recommended structure

1. Introduction

Define the problem and establish context.

2. Core theory

Explain the science/engineering principles.

3. Dairy application

Connect theory to actual processing.

4. Equipment/process

Use a flowchart or labelled diagram.

5. Quality & safety

Discuss relevant controls and limitations.

6. Conclusion + references

Summarize without simply repeating the introduction.
